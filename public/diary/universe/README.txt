UNIVERSE BACKGROUND VIDEOS
==========================

Place your high-definition galaxy/universe videos here:
- galaxy1.mp4
- galaxy2.mp4
- galaxy3.mp4
- galaxy4.mp4

The website will automatically use these videos as the rotating background.
If videos are not found, it will fall back to bright static images.

Recommended:
- Format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 or higher
- Duration: Any (will loop automatically)
- Bright, vibrant galaxy/nebula videos work best!
